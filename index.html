Abstract

Continuum robots can reach near an object without placing the gripper perfectly. Our vision-guided PPO policy controls reaching, while a support-enhanced granular-jamming gripper adapts to contact errors. Its flexible membrane and particles conform around the object, an internal rod promotes lateral enclosure, and vacuum locks the grasp for lift and transfer.
